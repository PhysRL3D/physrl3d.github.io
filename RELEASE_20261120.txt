Evidence boundaries
- Kangaroo footage is a selected physical release test, not a simulation.
- The support breakup animation is illustrative, not a physics prediction.
- Tree grams are slicer estimates, not scale measurements; purge/prime excluded.
- Benchmark means are not a claim about every individual example or metric.
- 20 endpoint pairs are included, including the SAS case with 11-degree tilt.
- Only the hydrant currently has a verified paired source replay. Its edited
  4-second source shows 0–2.96 simulation seconds, then holds the 9.96-second
  endpoint. The clock labels that jump. Other 19 cases are still-only.
- Display-mesh cutaways are not G-code toolpaths or physical infill.
- No new training, rerun simulation, artificial empirical video or texture
  inference is included in this release.
